## 1. Purpose & Strategic Intent
This Master Information Security Policy establishes executive leadership commitment and formal direction for information security across NexusPay Technologies Inc. ("NexusPay"). The objective of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) is to protect company assets, merchant transactions, customer personal data, and proprietary intellectual property against internal and external threats, whether deliberate or accidental.

## 2. Scope & Applicability
This policy applies to:
- All permanent employees, contractors, interns, and third-party vendors working for or on behalf of NexusPay.
- All software, cloud infrastructure (AWS), communication tools, internal networks, endpoints, databases, and customer-facing payment APIs operated by NexusPay.

## 3. Core Security Principles
1. **Confidentiality**: Customer payment data, credentials, and source code are restricted strictly to authorized individuals with a verified business need.
2. **Integrity**: Transaction processing pipelines and financial ledgers are protected against unauthorized modification, tampering, or deletion.
3. **Availability**: NexusPay payment services and critical operations maintain high resilience and meet contractual uptime agreements.

## 4. Roles and Governance Structure
- **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**:
  - Allocates budgetary and technical resources for the ISMS.
  - Formally approves major risk treatment decisions and policies.
- **Head of Information Security & GRC (Veera Ranjane)**:
  - Serves as the designated ISMS Management Representative.
  - Coordinates internal and external audits, tracks corrective action plans (CAPA), and monitors risk register remediation.
- **VP of Engineering & DevOps**:
  - Enforces secure software development lifecycle (SDLC) controls, branch protections, and infrastructure security configurations.
- **People Operations (HR)**:
  - Ensures all candidates undergo background checks and that joiner-mover-leaver (JML) notices are executed within policy SLAs.
- **All Workforce Members**:
  - Complete annual security awareness training.
  - Comply with all subsidiary policies and report suspected incidents within 1 hour of discovery.

## 5. Measurable Security Objectives (Clause 6.2)
- **Service Availability**: Maintain ≥ 99.9% uptime for production payment APIs.
- **Patch Management**: Remediate 100% of Critical CVEs within 7 calendar days and High CVEs within 14 calendar days.
- **Security Awareness**: Maintain 100% completion of security training for new hires within 14 days of onboarding.
- **Access Recertification**: Execute quarterly access reviews for all production and codebase systems with zero unauthorized accounts remaining active.

## 6. Disciplinary & Enforcement Provisions
Non-compliance with NexusPay security policies exposes the organization to reputational, legal, and regulatory harm. Any intentional circumvention, unauthorized disclosure, or willful negligence will result in disciplinary action up to immediate termination of employment and possible civil or criminal liability.
