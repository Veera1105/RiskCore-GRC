# Veera Ranjane | Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Portfolio

Welcome to my GRC portfolio repository. I specialize in aligning enterprise technical controls with international security frameworks, risk methodologies, and data privacy legislation.

My work bridges the gap between technical operations and business risk strategy—focusing on qualitative risk assessment, policy architecture, compliance gap analysis, and auditable governance structures.

### Portfolio Highlights & Real-World Projects

* **[ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS Policy Suite (NexusPay Technologies Inc.)](https://github.com/Veera1105/RiskLogic-GRC/tree/Projects/01-Governance/ISO-27001-NexusPay)**  
  *Path:* `Projects/01-Governance/ISO-27001-NexusPay/`  
  A complete governance architecture designed for an API-first FinTech payment orchestration platform. Encompasses scope definition (Clause 4), core policies (Access Control, AUP, BCDR), Annex A control mapping, and live audit evidence templates (Quarterly Access Reviews and Disaster Recovery Drills).

* **[Enterprise Risk Register & Assessment Matrix (ShopSphere GRC)](https://github.com/Veera1105/RiskLogic-GRC/tree/Projects/02-Risk-Management/ShopSphere-Risk-Register)**  
  *Path:* `Projects/02-Risk-Management/ShopSphere-Risk-Register/`  
  An end-to-end qualitative cybersecurity risk assessment for an e-commerce platform using NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 principles. Features an automated $3 \times 3$ Likelihood × Impact scoring engine, heat map prioritization, residual risk modeling across critical assets, and an executive dashboard built in Microsoft Excel.
