## Projects

### 1. IT Risk Assessment and Risk Register — Shopshere

**Project Type:** Simulated Organizational Risk Assessment  
**Organization:** Shopshere — A Hypothetical E-commerce Organization  
**Focus:** IT Risk Management, Risk Analysis, Risk Treatment

This project demonstrates the process of identifying, assessing, prioritizing, and documenting information security risks affecting a hypothetical e-commerce organization.

**Key Activities:**

- Identified potential information security risks involving customer data, access management, application security, and business operations.
- Developed a structured risk register documenting risk scenarios, affected assets, existing controls, likelihood, impact, and risk ratings.
- Applied a 3×3 likelihood-and-impact risk matrix to classify risks and support risk prioritization.
- Documented recommended risk treatment actions and mitigation measures to address identified risks.
- Structured risk documentation to support accountability, informed decision-making, and ongoing risk monitoring.

**Skills Demonstrated:** IT Risk Assessment, Risk Register Development, Risk Scoring, Risk Prioritization, Risk Treatment, Microsoft Excel.

### 2. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Policy Design

**Project Type:** Information Security Policy Documentation  
**Focus:** ISMS, Security Governance, Compliance Documentation

This project focuses on developing an information security policy for a hypothetical organization, using ISO/IEC 27001:2022 as a reference for information security management and governance practices.

**Key Activities:**

- Defined the policy's purpose, scope, objectives, and applicability to organizational information assets and personnel.
- Documented roles and responsibilities to establish accountability for information security.
- Developed policy requirements covering acceptable use, access control, and information security incident reporting.
- Incorporated policy review and maintenance considerations to support continued relevance and organizational oversight.
- Structured the document as a formal policy artifact to demonstrate practical security governance and compliance documentation skills.

**Skills Demonstrated:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISMS Fundamentals, Security Policy Development, Governance, Compliance Documentation.
