### Portfolio Highlights & Real-World Projects

#### 1. [ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS Policy Suite (NexusPay Technologies Inc.)](https://github.com/Veera1105/RiskCore-GRC/tree/main/Project%20-%201%20-%20ISO%2027001%20Policy%20Framework)
* **Scope & Context:** Scoped an enterprise ISMS framework under Clause 4 for a remote-first, API-first FinTech payment gateway operating across AWS multi-region infrastructure.
* **Access Control & Identity Governance:** Established strict access management baselines enforcing Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP), RBAC, WebAuthn/FIDO2 hardware-backed MFA via Okta, and JML onboarding/offboarding SLAs mapped to Annex A 5.15–5.18.
* **Policy Architecture:** Authored foundational operational policies covering Master Information Security, Acceptable Use (incorporating remote-work hygiene and Generative AI controls), and BCDR (defining RPO $\le$ 1 hr and RTO $\le$ 4 hrs).
* **Audit-Ready Evidence & Assurance:** Developed verifiable operational artifacts, including formal sign-off records for quarterly access recertifications and an annual disaster recovery failover drill test report mapped to Annex A 5.29–5.30 and 8.13–8.14.

#### 2. [Enterprise Risk Register & Assessment Matrix (ShopSphere GRC)](https://github.com/Veera1105/RiskCore-GRC/tree/main/Project%20-%202%20-%20Risk%20Register)
* **Methodology Alignment:** Conducted a comprehensive qualitative risk assessment following NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 principles, evaluating threat events against vulnerability exposures across critical e-commerce assets.
* **Risk Modeling & Inherent Scoring:** Evaluated 5 high-impact risk scenarios across Customer Databases, Payment APIs, Cloud Storage, and Production Servers using a standardized $3 \times 3$ Likelihood $\times$ Impact matrix.
* **Risk Treatment & Residual Reduction:** Formulated targeted control remediation roadmaps (e.g., privileged account MFA, API patching, bucket permission tightening, and immutable backups) that successfully eliminated 100% of High Inherent risks, reducing 4 to Medium and 1 to Low.
* **Technical Excel Implementation:** Automated scoring calculations and rating classifications using `VLOOKUP`, dynamic multi-condition formatting, and executive summary dashboards utilizing PivotTables and PivotCharts.
