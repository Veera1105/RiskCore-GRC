# GRC Case Studies & Portfolio

*Real-world applications of security frameworks, risk management, and privacy compliance.*

---

## 1. [Enterprise Risk Register: NIST SP 800-30](enterprise-risk-register)

* **Objective**: Identify, quantify, and prioritize information security risks for an enterprise entity following the **NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1** risk assessment framework.
* **GRC Focus**: Threat & Vulnerability Identification, Likelihood & Impact Analysis (3x3 Matrix), and Risk Treatment Strategies (Mitigate, Transfer, Accept, Avoid).
* **Outcome**: Developed Risk Register that mapped inherent risks to control recommendations, reducing critical inherent risk scores down to acceptable residual risk levels.

---

## 2. [ISO 27001 Policy Framework](iso27001-policy-framework)

* **Objective**: Establish a lean, foundational Information Security Management System (ISMS) for a cloud-native FinTech startup (**NexusPay Technologies Inc.**).
* **GRC Focus**: ISMS Governance, Access Control (PoLP / RBAC / JML), Endpoint AUP (MDM / GenAI Usage), and BCDR (RPO ≤ 1 hr, RTO ≤ 4 hrs).
* **Outcome**: Authored 4 foundational policies and operational audit evidence templates fully aligned with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** clauses and Annex A controls.
